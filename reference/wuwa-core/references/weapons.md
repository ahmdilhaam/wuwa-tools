## General Notes

- Sig weapons average 10–20% more DMG than best F2P
- CDmg secondaries (e.g. Verdant Summit, Wildfire Mark at 48.6%) pair well with characters who already have high CR from echoes
- CR secondaries (e.g. Red Spring, Ages of Harvest at 24.3%) let you invest more into CDmg in echoes
