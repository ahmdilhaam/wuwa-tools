### Verina — Support / Healer (Spectro)
**Source:** Rivyn Elowen — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=djzNyYkNKeg
- **Echo:** Bell-Borne Geochelone | 5pc Rejuvenating Glow
  - 4-cost main stat: Healing Bonus%
  - 3-cost: ATK% × 2
  - 1-cost: ATK% × 2
- **Weapons:** Stellar Symphony (BiS, sig) / Rectifier#25 or Comet Flare (F2P)
- **Stats:** ATK 1800+, ER% 150%+ (prioritize ER above all else), Healing Bonus% on 4-cost
- **Forte priority:** Forte Circuit (1st — heart of her healing) > Resonance Liberation > Resonance Skill
- **Substats:** ER% (Energy Regen) > ATK% > ATK flat > Resonance Liberation DMG Bonus
- **Teams:**
  - Universal flex slot — fits into virtually any team
  - Spectro teams: Verina + Jinhsi + Zhezhi (sub for Shorekeeper if no SK)
  - Frazzle support: Verina + Phoebe + Spectro Rover
- **Rotation:** Swap in Verina when Concerto is fully charged → trigger Intro Skill (1 Photosynthesis stack) → Resonance Skill → Liberation → Outro → swap back to DPS
- **Sequences:**
  - S1: Outro heals next character 20% ATK every 5s for 30s (minor QoL)
  - S2: Adds extra Forte bar + 10 Concerto Energy — first node worth targeting, smooths rotation
  - S4: Grants 15% Spectro DMG Bonus to team — major upgrade for Spectro DPS teams
  - S6: Not required; S2 and S4 are the main stops
- **Notes:** Bell-Borne Geochelone is a near-universal echo inclusion because it provides a defensive shield AND 10% ATK buff. Verina's Rejuvenating Glow 4pc gives 15% team ATK whenever she heals — she triggers this constantly. Photosynthesis Energy mechanic: every Intro, Skill, Liberation, or charged Heavy Attack grants stacks; use them all before leaving field.

### Rover (Spectro) — Sub-DPS / Frazzle Support (Spectro)
**Source:** Krow — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cyDu4_hXo60
- **Echo:** Impermanence Heron | 5pc Moonlit Clouds (Sub-DPS/Buffer build)
  - OR: Eternal Radiance 5pc for Main DPS build
  - 4-cost main stat: ER% or Spectro DMG
  - 3-cost: Spectro DMG% × 2
  - 1-cost: ATK% × 2
- **Weapons:** Emerald of Genesis (BiS F2P, best ER) / Blazing Brilliance (premium damage-oriented)
- **Stats:** ER% 130%+ (until comfortable), then CRIT Rate 60-70% : CRIT DMG 140%+
- **Forte priority:** Resonance Liberation (1st — primary Frazzle stack source) > Forte Circuit > Resonance Skill
- **Substats:** ER% (to breakpoint) ≥ CRIT Rate = CRIT DMG > ATK% > Flat ATK
- **Teams:**
  - Frazzle Core: Spectro Rover + Phoebe + Shorekeeper/Verina
  - Frazzle Core: Spectro Rover + Zani + Shorekeeper/Verina
  - Frazzle Triple: Spectro Rover + Phoebe + Zani (double DPS variant)
- **Rotation:** Intro Skill (fills Forte Gauge to 50% instantly) → Enhanced Resonance Skill → Basic Attacks to fill Forte → Liberation (main Frazzle applier) → Outro → swap to main DPS
- **Sequences:**
  - S1: +40% Resonance Liberation DMG Bonus — solid personal damage boost
  - S2: Reduces enemy Spectro RES by 10% for 20s — team-wide damage buff, excellent value
  - S3+: Diminishing returns; S2 is the sweet spot for F2P investment
- **Notes:** One of only three characters that can apply Spectro Frazzle (alongside Phoebe and Ciaccona). Mandatory in Phoebe and Zani teams. Free character — all players have them. Wavebands obtainable through in-game progression. Intro Skill's Forte fill trick is crucial for efficient rotations.

### Jinhsi — Main DPS (Spectro)
**Source:** Gacha Gamer — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6G5joY54cQM
- **Echo:** Jué (BiS, use before burst rotation for 16% Resonance Skill DMG bonus) / Mourning Aix (alt) | 5pc Celestial Light
  - 4-cost main stat: CRIT DMG or CRIT Rate
  - 3-cost: Spectro DMG% × 2
  - 1-cost: ATK% × 2
- **Weapons:** Ages of Harvest (sig BiS — CRIT Rate substat frees up echo budget) / Lustrous Razor or Verdant Summit (5-star standard alts)
- **Stats:** CRIT Rate 70%+, CRIT DMG 200%+, ER% ~120%, Resonance Skill DMG Bonus substats
- **Forte priority:** Forte Circuit (1st — ~74% of total damage via Illuminous Epiphany) > Resonance Liberation (~18%) > Resonance Skill > Basic Attack > Intro
- **Substats:** CRIT Rate = CRIT DMG > Resonance Skill DMG Bonus > ER% (to 120%) > ATK%
- **Teams:**
  - Best: Jinhsi + Zhezhi + Shorekeeper (Zhezhi stacks Incandescence at 2x efficiency via Coordinated Attacks + Resonance Skill DMG Outro)
  - Alt: Jinhsi + Yinlin + Shorekeeper (quickswap-friendly)
  - F2P: Jinhsi + Spectro Rover + Verina
- **Rotation:** Swap support (Zhezhi/Yinlin) to stack Incandescence → swap Jinhsi in via Intro → use Resonance Skill to proc Incarnation → stack Incandescence via Forte → cast Illuminous Epiphany (Forte heavy attack) → Liberation → repeat
- **Sequences:**
  - S1: Major damage boost — worth pulling for (increases Resonance Skill DMG significantly)
  - S2: Strong early node; S1+S2 together are highly recommended
  - S5: Another significant damage increase
  - S6: Endgame high-investment
- **Notes:** Jinhsi is unique in that her scaling is primarily Resonance Skill DMG, not ATK — so ATK% is less valuable than usual. Jué echo must be activated BEFORE Jinhsi's rotation window, not during. Incandescence mechanic: each Coordinated Attack from off-field allies grants Incandescence stacks; at max stacks, Illuminous Epiphany becomes available. Zhezhi doubles stack generation rate, making her the premier Jinhsi partner.

### Shorekeeper — Support / Buffer / Healer (Spectro)
**Source:** Gacha Gamer — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqHhEjhbNR8
- **Echo:** Fallacy of No Return (main — Spectro HP-scaling DMG + 10% ER + 10% team ATK for 20s) | 5pc Rejuvenating Glow
  - 4-cost main stat: HP%
  - 3-cost: HP% × 2
  - 1-cost: HP% × 2
- **Weapons:** Stellar Symphony (sig BiS — +12% HP + massive ER, makes 250% ER target easy) / Variation (craftable 4-star, best F2P — strong ER + Concerto restore)
- **Stats:** ER% 250% total (key breakpoint — unlocks full 12.5% CRIT Rate + 25% CRIT DMG team buff from Stellarealm); HP% on echoes for healing scaling
- **Forte priority:** Intro Skill (1st) > Resonance Liberation > Resonance Skill > Forte Circuit > Basic Attack
- **Substats:** ER% (to 250% total) > HP% > HP flat
- **Teams:**
  - Universal slot: Shorekeeper fits any team that can use her CRIT buffs
  - Jinhsi team: Jinhsi + Zhezhi + Shorekeeper
  - Frazzle team: Zani/Phoebe + Spectro Rover + Shorekeeper
  - Liberation team: Xiangli Yao + Lynae + Shorekeeper
- **Rotation:** Intro → Resonance Skill (place Dream Bubble) → Liberation (deploy Stellarealm field) → swap immediately — her value is ALL in her kit buffs, minimal field time needed
- **Sequences:**
  - S1: Extends Stellarealm duration + boosts healing — QoL upgrade
  - S2: Provides additional CRIT buff layers to the Stellarealm — strong investment
  - S0 is already one of the best supports in the game; sequences are luxury
- **Notes:** The 250% ER breakpoint is non-negotiable — below it, she loses the CRIT buffs that make her exceptional. With Stellar Symphony + Variation substat + Fallacy echo ER bonus, 250% is achievable. She is widely considered the best support in WuWa. Her Stellarealm field buffs the whole team with CRIT Rate and CRIT DMG simultaneously, making her uniquely powerful in any setup. HP scaling on healing means HP% echoes double as a scaling stat.

### Phoebe — Main DPS / Hybrid Sub-DPS (Spectro)
**Source:** IWinToLose Gaming — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4c9LY9q_N44
- **Echo:** 5pc Eternal Radiance (DPS build — Absolution state) | 5pc Moonlit Clouds (Sub-DPS — Confession state)
  - 4-cost main stat: CRIT DMG or CRIT Rate
  - 3-cost: Spectro DMG% × 2
  - 1-cost: ATK% × 2
- **Weapons:** Luminous Hymn (sig BiS — specifically buffs Basic/Heavy ATK when Frazzle is applied) / Stringmaster / Rime-Draped Sprouts / Cosmic Ripples / Augment / Ocean's Gift (F2P alts in descending order)
- **Stats:** CRIT Rate 60-70%, CRIT DMG 140%+, ER% ~120%
- **Forte priority:** Forte Circuit > Resonance Liberation > Resonance Skill > Basic Attack > Intro
- **Substats:** CRIT Rate = CRIT DMG > ER% (to 120%) > ATK%
- **Teams:**
  - Casual Best: Phoebe + Spectro Rover + Sanhua (Sanhua Outro +38% Basic ATK, fast rotation)
  - Premium: Phoebe + Spectro Rover + Shorekeeper
  - Absolution DPS: Phoebe + Mortefi + Spectro Rover (Rejuvenating Glow SK variant)
- **Rotation:** Swap in Phoebe via Intro → Confession state: cast Starflash to apply 5 stacks Frazzle quickly → switch to Absolution state → consume Frazzle stacks via Starflash for 256% Heavy ATK DMG Amplify → Liberation → swap out
- **Sequences:**
  - S1: Strong — increases Divine Voice cap, enabling more Absolution casts
  - S2: Very good — reduces rotation time by lowering Divine Voice cost
  - F2P at S0 is viable; S1+S2 are quality-of-life upgrades that noticeably improve flow
- **Notes:** Phoebe has two distinct states — Absolution (personal DPS, consumes Frazzle for massive Heavy ATK amp) and Confession (Frazzle applier/sub-DPS, applies 5 stacks per Starflash). Spectro Rover is MANDATORY in any Phoebe team — they provide the Frazzle Phoebe needs AND Spectro RES shred. Without Frazzle on enemies, Absolution's damage amplification does not activate. Sanhua is the budget pick because her Outro (+38% Basic ATK) directly buffs Phoebe's Starflash and her simple kit keeps rotations tight.

### Zani — Main DPS (Spectro)
**Source:** IWinToLose Gaming — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Lk2KuJ0BxRc
- **Echo:** 5pc Eternal Radiance (BiS — 10% Spectro DMG + 20% CRIT Rate + 15% Spectro DMG Bonus) | 5pc Celestial Light (alternative while farming)
  - 4-cost main stat: CRIT DMG or CRIT Rate
  - 3-cost: Spectro DMG% × 2
  - 1-cost: ATK% × 2
- **Weapons:** Blazing Justice (sig BiS — ATK + Spectro Frazzle DMG + DEF ignore on enemies) / Tragicomedy (strong alt — Heavy ATK DMG Bonus)
- **Stats:** ER% 115-120%+, CRIT Rate 50-70%, CRIT DMG 140%+ ; Heavy Attack DMG Bonus is a priority substat
- **Forte priority:** Resonance Liberation (1st — Inferno Mode gate and primary Frazzle conversion) > Forte Circuit > Resonance Skill
- **Substats:** CRIT Rate = CRIT DMG > Heavy Attack DMG Bonus > ATK% > ER% (to 115-120%)
- **Teams:**
  - Premium: Zani + Phoebe + Shorekeeper
  - F2P: Zani + Spectro Rover + Verina/Baizhi
  - Double DPS: Zani + Phoebe + Spectro Rover
- **Rotation:** Zani enters → Basic Attacks to build Redundant Energy → cast Resonance Liberation (enter Inferno Mode) → Heavy Slash combos to generate Spectro Frazzle → Frazzle stacks convert to Heliacal Embers for bonus damage → Liberation ends → swap to support
- **Sequences:**
  - S1: Significant — increases Spectro Frazzle DMG conversion rate in Inferno Mode
  - S2: Good sustained DPS increase
  - Worth pulling S1 if invested; S0 is functional
- **Notes:** Inferno Mode is the core of Zani's kit — must have Liberation available every rotation, hence the 115-120% ER minimum. Spectro Frazzle on enemies is required to trigger Heliacal Embers damage conversion. Eternal Radiance's 20% CRIT Rate bonus means she needs less CRIT Rate from substats, freeing rolls for CRIT DMG and Heavy ATK DMG. DEF ignore on Blazing Justice is rare and extremely powerful.

### Lynae — Sub-DPS / Hybrid Buffer (Spectro)
**Source:** IWinToLose Gaming — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jl91tCo2hcI
- **Echo:** Hyvatia (main, grants 10% All-Type DMG Bonus to next resonator's Intro) | 5pc Pact of Neonlight Leap (BiS Sub-DPS — Outro gives 15% additional ATK to next resonator via Tune Break Boost)
  - Fallback: 5pc Moonlit Clouds + Impermanence Heron (solid while farming)
  - DPS build: 5pc Rite of Gilded Revelation
  - 4-cost main stat: CRIT DMG or CRIT Rate
  - 3-cost: Spectro DMG% or ATK% × 2
  - 1-cost: ATK% × 2
- **Weapons:** Spectrum Blaster (sig BiS — 24% team All DMG on Tune Rupture/Tune Strain stacks) / Phasic Homogenizer (5-star standard, strong Spectro DMG passive) / Static Mist (better pure Outro buffing)
- **Stats:** ER% 120-230% (varies by team), CRIT Rate 50-80%, CRIT DMG 250-280%, ATK 2000+
- **Forte priority:** Forte Circuit (1st — enables entire kit) > Resonance Liberation (Visual Impact = major damage)
- **Substats:** ER% (to 120% minimum, up to 230% in some teams) > CRIT Rate = CRIT DMG > Basic Attack DMG Bonus > ATK%
- **Teams:**
  - Premiere: Lynae + Luuk Herssen + Mornye (Tune Break synergy triple)
  - Liberation teams: Lynae + Iuno + Shorekeeper
  - Liberation: Lynae + Xiangli Yao + Shorekeeper
- **Rotation:** Intro → Basic Attacks to build Overflow stacks → Polychrome Leap (Forte) → Visual Impact (Liberation) → Outro (buffs next resonator ATK) → swap to DPS
- **Sequences:**
  - S1: ~9% damage increase + interruption immunity during Polychrome Leap and Visual Impact — comfort upgrade, not essential
  - S2: BEST early node — +25% All DMG Amplification to Lynae AND Outro now gives 25% All DMG Amplification to incoming resonator. Both DPS and support output jump noticeably.
  - S3: Meaningful; S6 is for high investors
- **Notes:** Lynae's Outro buffing is her primary team value in sub-DPS builds. Tune Break Boost is her unique mechanic — when she or a teammate triggers Tune Break on an enemy, the Pact of Neonlight Leap set amplifies the next resonator's ATK significantly. Spectrum Blaster's passive stacks 3× (24% total All DMG) from repeated Tune Rupture/Strain triggers, making it exceptionally strong in Tune-focused teams. S1 is NOT a priority pull despite large-sounding numbers.

### Lucy (Cyberpunk: Edgerunners Collab) — Main DPS (Spectro)
**Source:** IWinToLose Gaming — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aMk9CdXmiPg
- **Echo:** Adam Smasher (activates Shadow of Shattered Dreams passive — 35% Heavy ATK DMG when triggering Hack-Shifting) | 1pc Shadow of Shattered Dreams + 2pc Eternal Radiance + 2pc Celestial Light (hybrid set)
  - 4-cost main stat: CRIT DMG or CRIT Rate
  - 3-cost: Spectro DMG% × 2 (from the two 2pc sets)
  - 1-cost: ATK% × 2
- **Weapons:** Spectral Trigger (sig BiS — high base ATK + CRIT DMG substat + ATK% + Spectro DMG + Heavy ATK DMG bonus simultaneously) / Phasic Homogenizer (best F2P standard — high base ATK + CRIT DMG substat + ATK% + Spectro DMG)
- **Stats:** ER% ~120%, CRIT Rate 60-70%, CRIT DMG 150%+
- **Forte priority:** Resonance Liberation (1st — ~47% of total damage) > Basic Attack (~40%) > Forte Circuit (7%) > Resonance Skill (5%) > Intro
- **Substats:** CRIT Rate = CRIT DMG > ATK% > Heavy Attack DMG Bonus > ER% (to 120%)
- **Teams:**
  - Best: Lucy + Rebecca + Mornye/Shorekeeper (Hack-Shifting synergy with Rebecca)
  - Standard: Lucy + Phrolova + Shorekeeper (Heavy Attack team)
  - Flex: Lucy + Iuno or Mortefi + Verina
- **Rotation:** Enter via Intro → build TCP via Basic Attacks → cast Deadlock (enhanced Skill using TCP) → chain Basic Attacks to accumulate Root Access → spend Root Access → Liberation (47% of damage) → repeat TCP → swap out
- **Sequences:**
  - RC1 (S1): QoL + rotational improvement — excellent F2P-friendly upgrade
  - RC2 (S2): Further rotation smoothing — both RC1 and RC2 are strong early investments
  - RC6: High-spender tier, ridiculous scaling
  - RC0 is fully functional for F2P
- **Notes:** Lucy is a Cyberpunk: Edgerunners collab character from patch 3.4. Her unique mechanic is Hack (a Tune Rupture variant) and the special state Hack-Shifting. Adam Smasher as her main echo is an unusual choice — it deliberately breaks the standard 5pc set bonus in exchange for a 35% Heavy ATK DMG multiplier from Shadow of Shattered Dreams, which triggers every time she activates Hack-Shifting. The 2pc+2pc Spectro setup fills the remaining 4 echo slots. Rebecca is her collab partner and best teammate due to Hack-Shifting synergy. Phasic Homogenizer is genuinely close to signature in performance — low-spender friendly.
