### Yangyang — Hybrid / Sub-DPS (Aero)
**Source:** Prydwen.gg (last major update Patch 2.3)
- **Rarity/Weapon:** 4★ Sword
- **Echo:** Impermanence Heron | Moonlit Clouds (5pc)
  - 4-cost main stat: CRIT Rate (or CRIT DMG)
  - 3-cost: Aero DMG × 2
  - 1-cost: ATK% × 2
- **Weapons:**
  - BiS: Emerald of Genesis R1 (ATK 587, CRIT Rate 24.3%) — best all-round; ER + CRIT Rate + stackable ATK%
  - Alt: Blazing Brilliance R1 (ATK 587, CRIT DMG 48.6%) — 102% vs Emerald baseline
  - F2P: Lumingloss R5 (ATK 387, ATK 36.4%) — 83%; easy passive
  - F2P: Endless Collapse R5 (ATK 462, ATK 18.2%) — use if you need Energy Regen
  - F2P Quickswap: Lunar Cutter R5 (ATK 412, ATK 30.3%) — best for minimal field time builds
- **Stats (S6 recommended):** ATK 1800–2200+, ER 107%+, CR 60–80%, CD 210–260%
- **Forte priority:** Resonance Liberation > Resonance Skill > Forte Circuit > Basic Attack > Intro Skill
- **Substats:** ER (until breakpoint) >= CRIT Rate = CRIT DMG > ATK% > Flat ATK > Liberation DMG% > Basic DMG%
- **Teams:**
  - Best: Carlotta + Yangyang + Shorekeeper/Verina
  - Alt: Xiangli Yao + Yangyang + Shorekeeper/Verina (quickswap required)
  - Alt: Changli + Yangyang + Shorekeeper/Verina (quickswap required)
- **Rotation:**
  1. Basic P3 → Heavy Atk → Heavy Atk: Zephyr Song
  2. Ultimate (cancel endlag) → Basic P1 → Heavy Atk → Heavy Atk: Zephyr Song
  3. Skill (cancel endlag) → Jump (cancel skill endlag) → Mid-Air Atk: Feather Release → Outro
- **Sequences:**
  - S1: +15% Aero DMG Bonus after Intro — minimal impact
  - S2: Heavy Attack restores 10 extra Energy once per 20s — minor QoL
  - S3: Resonance Skill DMG +40%, expanded pull range — decent personal boost
  - S4: Feather Release DMG +95% — significant personal damage jump
  - S5: Wind Spirals DMG +85% — significant Liberation boost
  - S6: After Feather Release, team ATK +20% for 20s — critical team buff; Yangyang is not viable below S5 for buffing
  - Verdict: S0–S4 not worth it; S5–S6 is where she becomes viable as a buffer
- **Notes:**
  - Outro funnels 20 Resonance Energy over 5s to the next character — unique, doesn't expire on swap
  - ONLY viable in quickswap-focused teams; not recommended for casual players
  - Rotation is one of the fastest in the game (~5s, comparable to Sanhua)
  - Despite no amplification buff on Outro, S6 20% ATK buff makes her workable in quickswap hypercarry teams
