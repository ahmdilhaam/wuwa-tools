### Chixia — Main DPS / Dual DPS (Fusion)
**Source:** Prydwen.gg / Game8.co / Wutheringlab (cross-referenced)
- **Echo:** Nightmare: Inferno Rider | Molten Rift (5pc)
  - 4-cost main stat: CRIT Rate (or CRIT DMG)
  - 3-cost: Fusion DMG × 2
  - 1-cost: ATK% × 2
- **Weapons:**
  - BiS: The Last Dance (ATK 500, CRIT DMG 72%) — Carlotta's sig; best for Skill-focused builds (108%)
  - Standard: Static Mist (ATK 587, CRIT Rate 24.3%) — calculation baseline (100%)
  - F2P best: Relativistic Jet R5 (ATK 462, ATK% 18.2%) — removes ER pressure (82%)
  - F2P alt: Undying Flame R5 (ATK 412, ATK% 30.3%) — Skill DMG on Intro (81%)
- **Stats:** ATK 1900–2100+, ER 115%+, CR 55–80%, CD 210–280%, Fusion DMG Bonus 40–70%
- **Forte priority:** Forte Circuit > Res. Liberation > Basic Attack > Res. Skill > Intro Skill
- **Substats:** ER (until 115%) >= CRIT Rate = CRIT DMG > ATK% > Flat ATK
- **Teams:**
  - Best Team: Chixia + Brant + Encore + Lupa (Mono Fusion)
  - Brant Dual DPS: Brant + Chixia + Shorekeeper / Verina
  - Changli Team: Changli + Chixia + Shorekeeper / Verina
- **Rotation:**
  1. Warm-up: Use 2x Resonance Skill charges off-field (swap-cancel), building ~60 Thermobaric Bullets
  2. Echo pre-combo (Inferno Rider placed before Intro; Nightmare variant — anytime pre-combo)
  3. Intro
  4. Skill: DAKA DAKA! — channel, then tap Basic at exactly 30 bullets to fire BOOM BOOM
  5. Ultimate (Blazing Flames)
  6. Skill: DAKA DAKA! again — full channel into BOOM BOOM
  7. Outro
- **Sequences:**
  - S1: Boom Boom always crits — solid damage (~5%), high early priority
  - S2: Ult recovers 5 energy per kill (up to 20) — situational in mob content
  - S3: Ult deals 40% more to targets below 50% HP — execute synergy
  - S4: Ult grants 60 Thermobaric Bullets + resets Skill CD — best sequence; major QoL + damage (~8%)
  - S5: At max Numbingly Spicy! stacks, ATK +30% — tied with S4 in calcs; no added value over S4
  - S6: Boom Boom grants team 25% Basic ATK DMG Bonus for 15s — strong with Brant/Encore teammates
  - Priority: S4 > S1 > S6 (if running Basic ATK DPS alongside)
- **Notes:**
  - Free 4-star. Energy cost 150 — tied for highest among DPS characters; ER 115%+ is non-negotiable.
  - Cannot parry (pistol class). Interruption during DAKA DAKA! causes massive damage loss — distance management required.
  - Exactly 30 bullets before BOOM BOOM is the sweet spot; over-channeling wastes resources for next cycle.
  - Inferno Rider (non-Nightmare) gives Basic ATK DMG bonus instead of Skill DMG but requires Swap Cancel after 3rd hit.

### Encore — Main DPS (Fusion)
**Source:** Prydwen.gg / Game8.co / Wutheringlab (cross-referenced)
- **Echo:** Inferno Rider (harder/stronger) or Nightmare: Inferno Rider (easier) | Molten Rift (5pc)
  - Alt set: Flaming Clawprint (5pc) with Lioness of Glory echo — use in Dual DPS teams (Changli/Brant) for 15% party Fusion DMG Bonus
  - 4-cost main stat: CRIT DMG > CRIT Rate
  - 3-cost: Fusion DMG > ATK% (pick better substats)
  - 1-cost: ATK% × 2
- **Weapons:**
  - BiS: Stringmaster R1 (ATK 587, CRIT Rate 24.3%) — Yinlin's sig; best overall (110%)
  - Alt: Rime-Draped Sprouts R1 (ATK 587, CRIT DMG 48.6%) — Zhezhi's sig (104%)
  - Alt: Whispers of Sirens R1 (ATK 587, CRIT DMG 48.6%) — Cantarella's sig (104%)
  - Standard (perm): Cosmic Ripples R1 (ATK 587, ATK% secondary) — best non-sig (100% baseline)
  - F2P: Radiant Dawn R5 Battle Pass (CRIT DMG + ATK + Basic ATK DMG on Skill cast) (94%)
  - F2P: Augment R5 Battle Pass (ATK + CRIT Rate) (94%)
  - F2P no-gacha: Jinzhou Keeper R5 (ATK boost on Intro) (83%)
- **Stats:** ATK 2000–2400+, ER 105–130% (lower w/ optimized rotation), CR 60–75%+, CD 215–285%+, Fusion DMG 40–70%+
- **Forte priority:**
  - Hypercarry: Res. Liberation > Forte Circuit > Res. Skill > Intro Skill > Basic Attack
  - Quick Swap: Res. Skill > Forte Circuit > Res. Liberation > Intro Skill > Basic Attack
- **Substats:** ER (until satisfied) > CRIT Rate = CRIT DMG > ATK% > ATK > Basic DMG% > Skill DMG%
- **Teams:**
  - Best Team: Encore + Lupa + Mornye / Shorekeeper (Mono Fusion Hypercarry)
  - Brant Dual DPS: Encore + Brant + Lupa + Shorekeeper / Mornye
  - With Sanhua: Encore + Sanhua + Shorekeeper / Verina
  - Dual DPS: Encore + Changli + Shorekeeper / Verina
- **Rotation (Easy Burst — recommended):**
  1. Echo (Inferno Rider: swap-cancel after 3rd hit; Nightmare: anytime pre-combo)
  2. Intro
  3. Ultimate (Cosmos Rave — enter enhanced form)
  4. Skill: Cosmos Rampage
  5. Basic 1–4 (Cosmos: Frolicking)
  6. Skill: Cosmos Rampage
  7. Basic 1–4
  8. Skill: Cosmos Rampage
  9. Heavy: Cosmos Rupture (Swap-Cancel the instant she begins channeling)
  10. Outro
  - Advanced: Skip Forte Heavy; do 3rd Cosmos Rampage + extra Basics via Dash/Skill animation cancels on Basic 4.
  - Cast Skill before Ultimate in warm-up to maintain Molten Rift set bonus active during Ult.
- **Sequences:**
  - S1: +3% Fusion DMG per Basic hit (4 stacks, 6s) — minor buff
  - S2: +10 Resonance Energy on Basic/Energetic Welcome (1/10s) — ER QoL
  - S3: Cloudy Frenzy / Cosmos Rupture multiplier +40% — significant personal damage
  - S4: Cosmos Rupture grants team 20% Fusion DMG Bonus for 30s — high value team buff
  - S5: Resonance Skill DMG Bonus +35% — good for Skill-heavy rotations
  - S6: During Ult, +5% ATK per hit (6 stacks, 10s) — strong sustained self-buff
  - Priority: S4 > S3 > S6
- **Notes:**
  - Versatile — Hypercarry or Quick-Swap. One of easiest 5-star DPS to play.
  - Lupa's Outro (20% Fusion Deepen + 25% Basic ATK Deepen) applies to both Encore's main damage types — exceptional synergy.
  - Mornye as 3rd slot enables Encore Hypercarry while activating full Lupa Mono Fusion buffs — strongest team core.
  - Forte Heavy during Cosmos Rave has a small input window before swap; vulnerability exists just after the heavy.

### Mortefi — Hybrid Sub-DPS / Support (Fusion)
**Source:** Prydwen.gg / Game8.co / Wutheringlab (cross-referenced)
- **Echo:** Impermanence Heron (swap-cancel just before Outro) | Moonlit Clouds (5pc)
  - Moonlit Clouds 5pc: 10% ER + 22.5% ATK to next char on Outro
  - Alt set: Empyrean Anthem (5pc) — only better than Moonlit Clouds with 9–10 CRIT substats + 5-star weapon
  - Alt main echo for Empyrean Anthem: Hecate (40% Coordinated ATK DMG in main slot)
  - 4-cost main stat: CRIT Rate (or CRIT DMG)
  - 3-cost: Fusion DMG × 2
  - 1-cost: ATK% × 2
- **Weapons:**
  - BiS: The Last Dance R1 (ATK 500, CRIT DMG 72%) — best for personal + hybrid damage (106%)
  - Standard: Static Mist R1 (ATK 587, CRIT Rate 24.3%) — CRIT + ER + ATK buff to next char; used in calculations (100%)
  - F2P: Relativistic Jet R5 (ATK 462, ATK% 18.2%) — removes most ER requirements (84%)
  - F2P alt: Novaburst R5 (ATK 412, ATK% 30.3%) — requires 3 initial dodges (84%)
- **Stats:** ATK 1900–2200+, ER 115–130% (S2: ~120%; Augusta team: ~113%), CR 60–80%, CD 215–260%+, Fusion DMG 40–70%+
- **Forte priority:** Res. Liberation > Forte Circuit > Basic Attack > Res. Skill > Intro Skill
- **Substats:** ER (until breakpoint) >= CRIT Rate = CRIT DMG > ATK% > Flat ATK
- **Teams:**
  - Best Team: Augusta + Mortefi + Shorekeeper / Verina
  - Galbrena Mono Fusion: Galbrena + Mortefi + Lupa
  - Jiyan Team: Jiyan + Mortefi + Shorekeeper / Verina
  - Phoebe Team: Phoebe + Mortefi + Spectro Rover (Rejuvenating Glow)
  - Zani Team: Zani + Mortefi + Spectro Rover (Rejuvenating Glow)
- **Rotation:**
  1. Intro
  2. Skill: Passionate Variation (unlocks extra Annoyance per Basic for 5s)
  3. Skill: Fury Fugue (Forte — fire immediately after Passionate Variation)
  4. Basic P1–P4 (within 5s window for bonus Annoyance)
  5. Skill: Fury Fugue (second Forte — no CD)
  6. Ultimate: Violent Finale (apply Burning Rhapsody — use when ~20 Concerto from cap)
  7. Echo: Impermanence Heron (Swap-Cancel immediately)
  8. Outro
  - Burning Rhapsody fires Marcato on ally Basic hits (×1) and Heavy hits (×2) for next character on field.
  - Always pair Outro with the start of a Heavy ATK DPS's burst window for maximum value.
- **Sequences:**
  - S1: Coordinated ATK on ally Resonance Skill (2 Marcatos) — solid damage boost (~5%)
  - S2: Echo Skill +10 Resonance Energy (1/20s) — ER QoL
  - S3: Marcato CRIT DMG +30% during Burning Rhapsody — ~9.5% dmg gain
  - S4: Burning Rhapsody duration +7s — extends buff window significantly; S3+S4 together ~52% team dmg increase
  - S5: Skill/Fury Fugue fire 4 extra Marcatos at 50% dmg — personal damage
  - S6: Ult grants team 20% ATK for 20s — strong team-wide buff
  - Priority: S3 → S4 (largest jump at S3+S4 combined) > S6 > S1
- **Notes:**
  - Outro: 38% Heavy ATK DMG Amplify for 14s — defining value; tailor-made for Augusta, Galbrena, Jiyan.
  - Moonlit Clouds + Impermanence Heron stacks 22.5% ATK + 12% DMG onto Outro target — triple buff layer.
  - S0 fully functional and excellent. S3+S4 represent a dramatic tier upgrade.
  - Ultimate CD is 20s vs 10s buff duration — plan rotation carefully; buff the main DPS's burst on every other rotation if needed.

### Changli — Hybrid DPS (Fusion)
**Source:** Prydwen.gg / Game8.co (cross-referenced)
- **Echo:** Nightmare: Inferno Rider | Molten Rift (5pc)
  - 4-cost main stat: CRIT Rate or CRIT DMG (pick better substats)
  - 3-cost: Fusion DMG + ATK% (or double Fusion DMG)
  - 1-cost: ATK% × 2
- **Weapons:**
  - BiS: Blazing Brilliance R1 (Changli's Signature Sword — CRIT DMG secondary + scaling Skill DMG Bonus) (100%)
  - Standard (near-BiS): Emerald of Genesis R1 (high ATK, CRIT Rate + ER + ATK on Skill) — best perm option; 91% of sig; NOT sig-dependent
  - Alt: Emerald Sentence R1 (CRIT Rate + team Echo Skill DMG; no ER) (90%)
  - F2P: Somnoire Anchor R5 (old event — scaling ATK + CRIT Rate) (81%)
  - F2P no-gacha: Commando of Conviction R5 (ATK stat-stick) (77%)
- **Stats:** ATK 2000–2200+, ER 108–120%, CR 65–80%+, CD 210–260%+, Fusion DMG 40–70%+
- **Forte priority:** Res. Skill > Forte Circuit > Res. Liberation > Basic Attack > Intro Skill
  - Basic and Intro can be deprioritized at minimal loss to save resources
- **Substats:** ER (until satisfied) > CRIT Rate = CRIT DMG > ATK% > ATK > Resonance Skill DMG%
- **Teams:**
  - Best Team: Changli + Aemeath + Brant + Lupa (Quickswap Mono Fusion)
  - Alt Best: Changli + Encore + Brant + Lupa
  - Brant Dual DPS: Changli + Brant + Shorekeeper / Verina
  - Expert Teams: Changli + Jinhsi; Changli + Xiangli Yao; Changli + Cartethyia (all with SK/Verina)
  - Dual Fusion: Changli + Encore / Chixia + SK / Verina
- **Rotation (Standard — single Intro):**
  1. Opener (if Ult ready at fight start): Ultimate → Heavy: Flaming Sacrifice (swap) — free damage
  2. Intro → True Sight: Charge (mid-air Basic)
  3. Skill (True Sight: Capture #1) → Heavy Attack → True Sight: Charge
  4. Basic: Mid-air 4 → True Sight: Charge
  5. Skill (True Sight: Capture #2) → True Sight: Conquest
  6. Heavy: Flaming Sacrifice (4 Enflamement consumed)
  7. Ultimate → Heavy: Flaming Sacrifice (swap-cancel)
  8. Outro
  - All key abilities can be Swap-Cancelled: Basic 4, Skill, True Sight Conquest, Forte Heavy.
  - Double Intro approach: skip Basic chain entirely — requires sacrificing one teammate's Intro but generates stacks faster.
- **Sequences:**
  - S1: Skill/Forte Heavy +10% DMG + interruption resistance — solid small buff
  - S2: Enflamement grants +25% CRIT Rate for 8s — very strong; nearly eliminates CRIT Rate substat pressure
  - S3: Ultimate DMG +80% — large personal damage boost
  - S4: After Intro, team ATK +20% for 30s — excellent team-wide buff
  - S5: Forte Heavy multiplier +50% and DMG +50% — massive; doubles Forte Heavy damage
  - S6: Skill/Forte Heavy/Ult ignore additional 40% DEF — ultimate scaling boost
  - Priority: S2 > S4 > S5 > S6
- **Notes:**
  - Outro duration is 10s — unique; shortest in game alongside Lumi. Requires fast-rotating teammates to benefit.
  - Emerald of Genesis makes her completely Signature-independent — one of the few 5-stars this applies to.
  - Skill-based: floor-to-ceiling gap is very wide. Casual play significantly underperforms vs optimized Quickswap.
  - Changli's Outro (20% Fusion + 25% Liberation Deepen) is uniquely exploited by Chixia (uses both damage types).

### Brant — Hybrid DPS (Fusion)
**Source:** Prydwen.gg / Game8.co / Wutheringlab (cross-referenced)
- **Echo:** Dragon of Dirge (12% Basic ATK DMG + 12% Fusion DMG in main slot) | Tidebreaking Courage (5pc)
  - Tidebreaking Courage 5pc: requires 250% ER to activate 30% Attribute DMG Bonus
  - 4-cost main stat: CRIT Rate (or CRIT DMG)
  - 3-cost: Fusion DMG × 2 (or Fusion DMG + ATK%)
  - 1-cost: ATK% × 2
- **Weapons:**
  - BiS: Unflickering Valor R1 (Brant's Signature Sword — ER 77% secondary + 48% Basic ATK DMG Bonus; ATK scales with ER over 150% up to 280%) (100%)
  - Standard: Emerald of Genesis R1 (high ATK, CRIT Rate + ER + ATK on Skill) — best non-sig; solid
  - F2P: Somnoire Anchor R5 (event weapon — ATK + CRIT Rate stacks)
  - F2P no-gacha: Commando of Conviction R5 (pure ATK stat-stick)
- **Stats:** ATK scales dynamically with ER (up to +2600 via Unflickering Valor passive), ER 250% (mandatory for Tidebreaking Courage set activation), CR ~75%+, CD ~200%+
- **Forte priority:** Forte Circuit > Basic Attack = Res. Liberation > Res. Skill > Intro Skill
- **Substats:** ER (until 250%) > CRIT Rate = CRIT DMG > ER (above 250% for extra ATK scaling) > Basic ATK DMG% > ATK% > ATK
- **Teams:**
  - Mono Fusion Trio: Brant + Changli + Lupa (premier — Lupa buffs Brant → Brant buffs Changli → Changli buffs Lupa)
  - Best Dual DPS: Encore + Brant + Lupa + Mornye / Shorekeeper
  - Chixia Team: Brant + Chixia + Shorekeeper / Verina
  - Galbrena Mono Fusion: Galbrena + Brant + Lupa
- **Rotation:**
  1. Intro
  2. Basic Attack chain (mid-air — all Brant attacks are mid-air and Basic Attack type)
  3. Skill: Returned from Ashes (enhanced — Bravo gauge full; heavy Fusion hit + heals + shields team)
  4. Basic Attack chain (rebuild Bravo gauge)
  5. Skill again (when available)
  6. Ultimate (large Fusion nuke)
  7. Outro (25% Resonance Skill Deepen + 20% Fusion Deepen to next character for 14s)
  - All Brant attacks count as Basic Attacks — benefits from Lupa's Outro 25% Basic ATK Deepen.
  - Healing and shields are passive bonuses from his kit — reduces need for a dedicated healer.
- **Sequences:**
  - S1: Enhanced Skill DMG/healing bonuses — moderate personal damage
  - S2: ATK boost on Skill cast — ~15% increase; solid stat upgrade
  - S3: Ultimate multiplier increase — notable nuke upgrade
  - S4: Team ATK buff on Skill use — consistent team DPS increase
  - S5: Further Skill multiplier boost (~10%)
  - S6: Additional DMG amplification — endgame investment
  - Priority: S2 → S4 most impactful at lower investment
- **Notes:**
  - 250% ER threshold for Tidebreaking Courage is mandatory — without it, use Molten Rift instead.
  - Brant fights entirely in mid-air — immune to ground AoE, very mobile. Natural synergy with Changli and Lupa.
  - Self-sustain (heals + shields) makes him extremely space-efficient — can slot into teams that lack a healer.
  - Mono Fusion Trio (Brant + Changli + Lupa): each character's Outro buffs the next in a perfect closed loop.
  - Best 5-star partner for Chixia — his Outro buffs both of her primary damage types (Fusion + Skill DMG).

  - 4-cost main stat: CRIT Rate or CRIT DMG
  - 3-cost: Fusion DMG × 2 (or Fusion DMG + ATK%)
  - 1-cost: ATK% × 2

  - BiS: Wildfire Mark R1 (Lupa's Signature Broadblade — ATK 587, CRIT DMG 48.6%; +12% ATK; +48% Liberation DMG on Intro/Ult; team +24% Fusion DMG Bonus on extension) (100%)

  - F2P budget: Autumntrace R5 (ATK 412, CRIT Rate 20.2%) (78%)

  - Minor Fortes: CRIT Rate +8%, HP +12%
- **Forte priority:** Forte Circuit > Res. Liberation > Intro Skill > Res. Skill > Basic Attack
- **Substats:** CRIT Rate = CRIT DMG > ATK% > Res. Liberation DMG% > Flat ATK (ER 110–120% via substats if needed)
- **Teams:**
  - Mono Fusion Trio: Brant + Lupa + Changli (perfect Outro chain loop)
  - Mono Fusion w/ Encore: Encore + Lupa + Mornye / Shorekeeper
  - Mono Fusion w/ Galbrena: Galbrena + Brant + Lupa
  - Aemeath Fusion Burst: Aemeath + Denia + Lupa (Denia on Flaming Clawprint, Lupa on Moonlit Clouds)
  - Dual Fusion: Brant + Lupa + Shorekeeper / Mornye

  1. Intro Skill (or Enhanced Intro — becomes Ult-damage nuke at max Pack Hunt stacks)
  2. Resonance Skill × 2 (Shewolf's Hunt → Feral Fang; 2nd hit deals 50% more to marked target)
  3. Switch to other characters — build Concerto to 100 on teammates
  4. Intro Skill again (or Enhanced)
  5. Resonance Liberation (enters Burning Matchpoint; Wolflame = 100)
  6. Basic Attack → Foebreaker (mid-air)
  7. Mid-air Basic × 2
  8. Mid-air Basic 3 → Firestrike (consumes 50 Wolflame → Wolfaith ×1)
  9. Heavy: Wolf's Claw (Wolfaith ×2)
  10. Resonance Skill: Enhanced Dance with the Wolf (both Wolfaith consumed — major hit)
  11. Outro (20% Fusion Deepen + 25% Basic ATK Deepen for 14s to next character)

  - S2: 40% DMG increase to applicable skills for team — ~13.9% team dmg; high value
  - S3: Enhanced Intro doubled; Pack Hunt + Glory reach max value without Fusion character count requirements — ~20.5% dmg; excellent
  - S4: Enhanced Dance with the Wolf multiplier boost — ~23.4% personal dmg; very strong
  - S5: Minor improvement (~3.4%); low priority
  - S6: 3 core skills ignore 30% DEF; second Enhanced Dance unlocked immediately via Ult — ~26.2% standard / 71.2%+ in full double-Dance rotation
  - Priority: S2 > S3 > S4 > S6 (S1/S5 lower value)
- **Notes:**
  - Cornerstone of Mono Fusion. Outro (20% Fusion + 25% Basic ATK Deepen) defines team rotation order.
  - Triple Fusion: Pack Hunt reaches max 18% ATK + max 15% Fusion RES shred — both are multiplicative team multipliers.
  - Wildfire Mark's party-wide +24% Fusion DMG Bonus is ~10% total team DPS loss without it in Mono Fusion — sig is meaningful.
  - Wild Hunt state (3 Intros during Pack Hunt duration) upgrades her Intro to a 900%-multiplier Liberation nuke.
  - In Aemeath+Denia team: Lupa should run Moonlit Clouds (not Flaming Clawprint) so Denia can use Flaming Clawprint instead for optimal buff layering.

  - 3-cost: Fusion DMG × 2 (or Fusion DMG + ATK%)
  - 1-cost: ATK% × 2

  - BiS: Lux & Umbra R1 (Galbrena's Signature Pistols — tailor-made; Heavy ATK + Echo Skill DMG Amplify; DEF Ignore when both active) (100%)

  - F2P: Static Mist R1 (ATK 587, CRIT Rate 24.3%) — solid CRIT stat-stick
  - F2P 4-star: Thunderbolt R5 (ATK 387, ATK% 36.4%) — conditional Skill DMG stacks; requires consistent Basics
- **Stats:** ATK 2000+, ER ~120%, CR ~75%, CD ~250%, Heavy ATK DMG% valued as substat

  5. Outro

- **Sequences:**
  - S1: CRIT DMG boost on enhanced attacks + interruption immunity during final enhanced hit — ~48% personal dmg increase in BiS team; exceptional priority
  - S2: ATK bonus on Burning Drive buff +350% — ~23% personal dmg increase
  - S3: Liberation multiplier boost — ~20% personal dmg increase
  - Priority: S1 is the highest value sequence by far (48% dmg gain); very worth chasing
- **Notes:**
  - New best single-target Fusion DPS in game (as of patch 3.x). Excels vs Overlord class and boss content.
  - Flamewing's Shadow set is mandatory — her scaling specifically in this set dramatically outpaces alternatives.
  - Qiuyuan is her uniquely optimal partner — only character providing Echo Skill DMG buffs to teammates.
  - In Mono Fusion with Lupa: Lupa must be on Flaming Clawprint for the Fusion DMG team bonus.
