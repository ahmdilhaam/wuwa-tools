### Sanhua — Hybrid / Sub-DPS (Glacio)
**Source:** Prydwen.gg / Wutheringlab (cross-referenced)
- **Echo:** Impermanence Heron | Moonlit Clouds (5pc)
  - 4-cost main stat: CRIT DMG > CRIT Rate
  - 3-cost: Glacio DMG × 2
  - 1-cost: ATK% × 2
- **Weapons:** Blazing Brilliance (ATK 587, CRIT DMG 48.6%) / Emerald of Genesis (ATK 587, CRIT Rate 24.3%) / Commando of Conviction F2P (ATK 412, ATK 30.3%) / Lunar Cutter F2P (ATK 412, ATK 30.3%)
- **Stats:** ATK 1800–2200+, ER 100% (no extra ER needed on Moonlit Clouds set — 2pc gives 10% ER), CR 60–80%+, CD 210–260%+
- **Forte priority:** Forte Circuit > Res. Liberation > Res. Skill > Intro Skill > Basic Attack
- **Substats:** ER (until breakpoint, 0% extra on Moonlit) >= CR = CD > ATK% > Flat ATK
- **Teams:**
  - Best Team: Camellya + Sanhua + The Shorekeeper/Verina
  - Encore Team: Encore + Sanhua + The Shorekeeper/Verina
  - Brant Hypercarry: Brant + Sanhua + The Shorekeeper/Verina
  - Budget Havoc Rover: Rover (Havoc) + Sanhua + The Shorekeeper/Verina
  - Carlotta Budget: Carlotta + Sanhua + The Shorekeeper/Verina
- **Rotation:** Intro → Ultimate → Skill → Heavy Attack Detonate → Heavy Attack Detonate (×2 if pre-S5) → Echo swap-cancel → Outro. S5+ drops second Detonate: Intro → Ult → Skill → Detonate → Echo swap-cancel → Outro. Basic ATK variant: Intro → Basic P3/P4/P5 → Ult → Skill → Detonate → Outro.
- **Sequences:** S0 fully viable. S3: +35% DMG vs targets below 70% HP. S4: Ult restores 10 Energy + next Detonate +120% DMG — strong spike. S5: +100% CRIT DMG on Forte Ice Burst + Ice constructs explode without detonation — biggest single upgrade, enables shorter rotation. S6: +10% ATK ×2 stacks to team after detonation. Priority: S5 most impactful, S4 also strong.
- **Notes:** Free 4-star. Outro = 38% Basic ATK DMG Deepen for 14s (persists through swaps). Moonlit 5pc + Impermanence Heron stacks 22.5% ATK + 12% DMG + 38% Basic ATK Deepen on Outro target. Forte minigame: must release Heavy ATK in blue "Frostbite" zone to detonate — zone expands with Clarity stacks (gained from Intro, Skill, Ult, or Basic V). Outclassed in Carlotta teams by Zhezhi (5-star) and Yangyang (4-star). Near-equal to Roccia in Camellya teams despite being free.

### Baizhi — Support / Healer (Glacio)
**Source:** Prydwen.gg / Wutheringlab (cross-referenced)
- **Echo:** Fallacy of No Return (preferred) OR Bell-Borne Geochelone | Rejuvenating Glow (5pc). Alt: Impermanence Heron | Moonlit Clouds (5pc)
  - 4-cost main stat: Healing Bonus > HP%
  - 3-cost: Energy Regen × 2
  - 1-cost: HP% × 2
- **Weapons:** Stellar Symphony (ATK 412, ER 77%) / Variation F2P (ATK 337, ER 51.8%) / Call of the Abyss F2P free (ATK 338, ER 51.8%) / Rectifier of Voyager F2P (ATK 300, ER 32.3%)
- **Stats:** HP 20000–35000+, DEF 1100+, ER 220–230% (critical — 175 energy cost Ult), CR/CD only if running Moonlit Clouds variant
- **Forte priority:** Res. Liberation > Res. Skill > Forte Circuit > Intro Skill > Basic Attack
- **Substats:** ER (until ~220–230%) > HP% > Flat HP
- **Teams:**
  - Universal — fits almost any team in the Support slot
  - Incompatible with Coordinated Attack Hybrids (Yinlin, Zhezhi, Mortefi) — buffs only one character at a time
- **Rotation:** Intro → Basic P1/P2/P3/P4 → Skill (swap DPS to collect Euphonia feather) → Ultimate (delayed) → Basic P1/P2/P3 → Echo → Outro (~6.5s without concerto weapon; ~5.5s with R1 concerto weapon). S0R3 shortcut: Intro → Ult → Basic P1–P4 → Echo → Skill → Outro (~4.31s).
- **Sequences:** S1: Skill restores 2.5 Resonance Energy per Concentration — helps ER. S5: Revive one downed team member at 100% HP once per 10 min — high value in hard content. S6: Euphonia pickup grants all nearby characters +12% Glacio DMG Bonus for 20s. Priority: S5 for revive utility; S0 otherwise sufficient.
- **Notes:** Free 4-star. Outro: 15% all-type DMG Deepen for 6s + HP-based team healing for 30s (persists through swaps). Inherent Skill 1: Resonance Skill drops Euphonia feather on the ground — only one character can pick it up for +15% ATK for 20s; must deliberately route your DPS to the feather. Outclassed by Shorekeeper and Verina as generalists — best used as F2P budget support. Max Energy 175 = one of the highest in game; ER investment is critical.

### Lingyang — Main DPS (Glacio)
**Source:** Prydwen.gg / Wutheringlab (cross-referenced)
- **Echo:** Mech Abomination | Lingering Tunes (5pc) [Best — hypercarry only]. Alt: Sentry Construct | Frosty Resolve (5pc) [2nd best — viable in quickswap]. Alt: Lampylumen Myriad | Freezing Frost (5pc) [3rd — not recommended due to long cast time]
  - 4-cost main stat: CRIT DMG > CRIT Rate
  - 3-cost: Glacio DMG × 2 (or 1 Glacio DMG + 1 ATK%)
  - 1-cost: ATK% × 2
- **Weapons:** Moongazer's Sigil (ATK 500, CRIT Rate 36%) best / Abyss Surges non-sig BiS (ATK 587, ATK 36.4%) / Aether Strike F2P BP (ATK 412, CRIT DMG 40.5%) / Legend of Drunken Hero F2P free (ATK 462, ATK 18.2%)
- **Stats:** ATK 2100–2500+, ER 120–125%+ (estimated in Zhezhi+Shorekeeper team), CR 60–80%+, CD 215–260%+, Glacio DMG 40–70%+
- **Forte priority:** Forte Circuit > Res. Liberation > Intro Skill > Res. Skill > Basic Attack
- **Substats:** ER (until ~120–125%) >= CR = CD > ATK% > Flat ATK
- **Teams:**
  - Best Team: Lingyang + Lynae + Zhezhi + Mornye/Shorekeeper/Verina (Mornye only best with Lynae)
  - F2P Team: Lingyang + Sanhua + Shorekeeper/Verina
  - Zhezhi Team: Lingyang + Zhezhi + Shorekeeper/Verina
- **Rotation:** Echo (Mech Abomination before combo, instant) → Intro → Ultimate: Lion's Vigor → Heavy Attack: Glorious Plunge (enter airborne Forte) → alternate Basic Feral Gyrate / Skill Mountain Roamer ×4–5 rounds → Stormy Kicks → Mid-Air Tail Strike → Outro. Key rule: NEVER chain 2 Basics or 2 Skills in a row — always alternate to trigger Inherent Skill: Diligent Practice (+150% Mountain Roamer hit).
- **Sequences:** S0–S2 no meaningful damage increase. S3: +20% Basic ATK DMG + 10% Skill DMG during Ult. S4: Outro grants team +20% Glacio DMG Bonus for 30s — strong team-wide buff. S6: First 3s after Mountain Roamer, next Basic ATK +100% DMG Bonus — large personal gain. Priority: S4 for team buff value; S6 for personal DPS. S0 functional but lags behind modern DPS.
- **Notes:** Do NOT swap Lingyang during Ult burst window — causes major damage loss. Hypercarry archetype only. Forte gauge fills completely from just Intro + Ult — can skip Basic pre-ramp. Lingering Tunes requires staying on field (stacks over 6s) — incompatible with quickswap. Flying/airborne state can dodge some ground mechanics but makes dodging airborne attacks harder. Lower overall ceiling vs. newer 5-star DPS but fully playable.

### Zhezhi — Hybrid / Sub-DPS (Glacio)
**Source:** Prydwen.gg / Wutheringlab (cross-referenced)
- **Echo:** Nightmare: Lampylumen Myriad | Empyrean Anthem (5pc) [Best at endgame investment: 9–10 CRIT substats + 5-star weapon]. Alt: Impermanence Heron | Moonlit Clouds (5pc) [Best at average investment]
  - 4-cost main stat: CRIT DMG > CRIT Rate
  - 3-cost: Glacio DMG × 2 (or Glacio DMG + ATK%)
  - 1-cost: ATK% × 2
- **Weapons:** Rime-Draped Sprouts sig (ATK 412 base, CRIT DMG secondary, Basic DMG scaling buff) / Whispers of Sirens alt (95.8%) / Stringmaster alt (93.8%) / Cosmic Ripples F2P (ATK 587, scaling Basic DMG + ER, 84.6%) / Augment F2P BP (best 4-star, 81.1%)
- **Stats:** ATK 1900–2400+, ER 116–128%+ (116% on Moonlit Clouds; 128% on Empyrean Anthem in Carlotta+Shorekeeper team), CR 65–80%+ before echo bonuses, CD 215–260%+, Glacio DMG 30–70%+
- **Forte priority:** Res. Liberation > Forte Circuit > Basic Attack > Res. Skill > Intro Skill (Basic/Skill/Intro can be de-prioritized to save resources)
- **Substats:** ER (until satisfied) > CR = CD > ATK% > Basic DMG% > Flat ATK
- **Teams:**
  - Best (Carlotta): Carlotta + Zhezhi + Shorekeeper/Verina
  - Best (Jinhsi): Jinhsi + Zhezhi + Shorekeeper/Verina
  - Lingyang Fallback: Lingyang + Zhezhi + Shorekeeper/Verina
- **Rotation:** Intro → Basic 1/2/3 → Skill: Manifestation → Heavy: Conjuration → Skill: Stroke of Genius (Jump Cancel) → Skill: Stroke of Genius (cancel via Ult) → Ultimate → Skill: Creation's Zenith (Dash cancel into Echo on Moonlit Clouds; OR swap+Outro on Empyrean) → Outro. Jump Cancel on teleport Skills = key optimization to reduce rotation time at no damage cost.
- **Sequences:** S0 excellent — one of the best Hybrids at base. S1: Creation's Zenith +15 Resonance Energy restore + +10% CRIT Rate — helps ER. S4: Ult grants team +20% ATK for 30s — very strong. S5: Extra Inklit Spirit per 3 summoned — more off-field damage. Priority: S0 fully viable; S4 biggest team buff. Note: Prydwen flags "terrible sequence scaling" (version 1 character trait) — S nodes aren't as impactful as modern characters.
- **Notes:** Outro: 20% Glacio DMG Amplify + 25% Resonance Skill DMG Amplify for 14s (until swap) — critical buff for Carlotta and Jinhsi. Inherent Skill 2: Outro restores 15 Resonance Energy to incoming character — very valuable for energy-hungry DPS. Empyrean Anthem requires endgame gear to outperform Moonlit Clouds — use Moonlit Clouds at average investment. Off-field Inklit Spirits from Liberation continue attacking while DPS is active. Directly powercrept in Jinhsi teams by Luuk Herssen (3.x character) — check updated tier lists for Jinhsi pairing priority.

**Source:** Prydwen.gg / Wutheringlab (cross-referenced)
- **Echo:** Fallacy of No Return (preferred) OR Bell-Borne Geochelone | Rejuvenating Glow (5pc). Alt: Impermanence Heron | Moonlit Clouds (5pc)
  - 4-cost main stat: Healing Bonus
  - 3-cost: Energy Regen > ATK% × 2
  - 1-cost: ATK% × 2
- **Weapons:** Marcato F2P (ATK 337, ER 51.8%) — best by far / Gauntlets#21D (ATK 387, ER 38.8%) / Abyss Surges (ATK 587, ATK 36.4%) / Celestial Spiral (ATK 462, ATK 18.2%)
- **Stats:** HP 15000+, DEF 1050+, ATK 1300+, ER 100% (Marcato + Rejuv Glow 2pc handles this), CR 60–80%+ / CD 210–260%+ (only Moonlit variant)
- **Forte priority:** Res. Skill > Forte Circuit > Res. Liberation > Basic Attack > Intro Skill
- **Substats:** ER (until satisfied) = ATK% > Flat ATK
- **Teams:**
  - Best (Coordinated ATK): Calcharo + Yinlin + Youhu
  - Zhezhi Team: Lingyang + Zhezhi + Youhu
  - Mortefi Team: Jiyan + Mortefi + Youhu
- **Rotation (Rotation 1, 0–1 Auspice, ~6.5s with R1 Marcato):** Intro → Antique Appraisal (instant cancel) → Ultimate (choose Antique: Mask vs boss, Ruyi vs small enemies) → Antique Appraisal → Basic P1/P2/P3/P4 → Skill → Antique Appraisal → Outro. Rotation 2 (2–4 Auspice start, ~6.37s): Intro → Antique Appraisal (cancel) → Ult → Antique Appraisal → Forte: Poetic Essence (optional swap-cancel) → Basic P1 → Skill → Antique Appraisal (swap) → Outro.
- **Sequences:** S2: Doubles DMG bonus of Antithesis/Triplet/Perfect Rhyme on Poetic Essence — biggest personal damage upgrade. S3: +20% ATK permanently — improves healing and damage. S5: +15% CRIT Rate for 14s after Intro. S6: Up to +60% CRIT DMG from Sky Blue stacks on Antique Appraisal. Priority: S0 functional; S2 biggest upgrade.
- **Notes:** Outro: 100% Coordinated Attack DMG Amplify for 28s — largest single Coordinated ATK buff in game. Niche support — only competitive in teams with significant Coordinated ATK output (Yinlin, Zhezhi, Mortefi). Lucky Draw is random by default; only Liberation lets you choose — use Mask against bosses (AoE pull), Ruyi vs. small enemies (highest DMG). Rotations require Marcato weapon to hit listed times; other weapons require extra Basics and substantially more field time. Considered T4 by Prydwen in both endgame modes — competent but niche.

### Carlotta — Main DPS (Glacio)
**Source:** Prydwen.gg / Wutheringlab (cross-referenced, last updated Jun 28 2026)
- **Echo:** Sentry Construct | Frosty Resolve (5pc)
  - 4-cost main stat: CRIT Rate (22%) or CRIT DMG (44%)
  - 3-cost: Glacio DMG × 2
  - 1-cost: ATK% × 2 (18% ATK each)
- **Weapons:** The Last Dance sig (ATK 500, CRIT DMG 72.0%, +24% ATK + up to +96% Res. Skill DMG for 5s on Intro/Ult) / Static Mist alt (ATK 587, CRIT Rate 24.3%, 83% effectiveness) / Undying Flame F2P (ATK 412, ATK 30.3%, +40% Res. Skill DMG for 15s after Intro, 75%)
- **Stats:** ATK 2000–2500+, ER ~120–130%, CR 65%+, CD 200%+, Glacio DMG 30%+ (~90% of all damage is Resonance Skill type)
- **Forte priority:** Res. Liberation > Res. Skill > Forte Circuit > Basic Attack > Intro Skill (~53% Liberation / ~24% Skill / ~11% Forte)
- **Substats:** CR/CD > ATK% > Resonance Skill DMG Bonus > Flat ATK
- **Teams:**
  - Best Team: Carlotta + Zhezhi + Shorekeeper/Verina
  - Budget: Carlotta + Yangyang + Shorekeeper/Verina
  - F2P Budget: Carlotta + Sanhua + Shorekeeper/Verina (Sanhua barely buffs Carlotta but is usable)
- **Rotation:**
  1. Echo Skill (before rotation)
  2. Intro Skill (3 Moldable, 30 Substance)
  3. Heavy Attack (Crystal=6, Substance=60)
  4. Basic Attack ×3 via Necessary Measures (Crystal=0, Substance=90)
  5. Resonance Skill: Art of Violence (Crystal=6, Substance=90)
  6. Resonance Skill: Chromatic Splendor (Crystal=0, Substance=120)
  7. Mid-air Attack (Crystal=3, Substance=120)
  8. Heavy Attack: Imminent Oblivion (special red Tinted Crystal — 800%+ mult) → Grand Unveiling
  9. Resonance Liberation (Era of New Wave — 18% DEF ignore → Twilight Tango)
  10. Basic Attack: Death Knell ×4 → Fatal Finale
  11. Resonance Skill → Chromatic Splendor
- **Sequences:** S1: +30 Substance from Intro/Skill — fixes sig weapon 5s buff timing issue, allows Imminent Oblivion to benefit from the buff (~9.3% DMG, highly recommended with sig). S2: Liberation DMG multiplier ×2.26 (multiplicative with Final Bow ×1.8) — ~22% DMG. S3: Resonance Skill multiplier +70% — ~24% DMG (best single node). S4: ~7.2%. S5: ~3.5%. S6: Liberation multiplier increase, same multiplicative interaction as S2 — ~31.4% DMG. Priority: S1 (if using sig), S2, S3, S6.
- **Notes:** Single-target specialist — ~90% Resonance Skill type damage. NOT suited for multi-wave content. Tinted Crystal: activates every 22s — red crystal = special Heavy ATK (Imminent Oblivion, ~800% mult, 18% DEF ignore from Ult proc); blue crystal = only normal Heavy ATK (~200% mult). Always wait for red crystal. Final Bow buff: when Substance full, Ult DMG multiplier +80%; persists through character swap until Ult used. Frosty Resolve set is ~5–6% stronger than Freezing Frost for Carlotta (34.5% Glacio + 60% Res. Skill DMG vs. 52% Glacio + 12% Res. Skill DMG). Sig weapon buff only lasts 5s — at S0 the tail portion of Imminent Oblivion misses the window. S1 is specifically designed to fix this issue.

  - 1-cost: ATK% × 2

- **Stats:** HP 16000+, DEF 1150+, ATK 1900–2200+, CR 65–80%+, CD 210–280%+, ER 100% (Lucilla holds 0 Resonance Energy — zero ER investment needed), Glacio DMG 30–70%+
- **Forte priority:** Res. Liberation > Res. Skill > Forte Circuit > Intro Skill > Basic Attack (Basic can be completely skipped; Intro can be skipped at minimal loss)
- **Substats:** CR = CD > ATK% > ATK = Basic DMG% (Basic DMG% only relevant in Chafe mode; skip for Echo mode)
- **Teams:**
  - Glacio Chafe: Hiyuki + Lucilla + Chisa (Shorekeeper/Verina are weaker alternatives in 3rd slot)
  - Echo Skill Hypercarry: Sigrika + Lucilla + Shorekeeper/Verina (or Galbrena)
  - Phrolova Team: Phrolova + Lucilla + Qiuyuan + Cantarella (pivot Lucilla to 3rd with Cantarella)
- **Rotation:** Intro → Skill: Spotlight (Perfect Release — hold until lens arrows turn golden, ~0.25s after cast) → Ultimate: Clear As Day (unlocks when holding 3 Photos) → Hold Basic → Tracing Forms 1/2/3 (Oblivion procs during 3) → Letting It Go (cinematic finisher — uninterruptible, immune to damage, swap disabled) → Echo (timing by mode: Echo/Heron teams swap after Letting It Go before Outro; Chafe/Glommoth summon at any point) → Outro.
- **Sequences:** S1: Perfect Focus auto-expands on first landing + +20% CR after Spotlight + interruption immunity — major QoL + damage. S2: During Reminiscence — Chafe mode: +80% Glacio Chafe DMG Amp for 30s after Reminiscence ends; Echo mode: +40% Echo Skill DMG Amp to all team — very strong. S3: Letting It Go DMG ×2. S6: Up to +600% Letting It Go DMG from Remembrance stacks — whale territory. Priority: S1 first breakpoint; S2 biggest buff/damage increase.

Outro buffs: Chafe Mode → 60% Glacio Chafe DMG Amp for 30s (not lost on swap); Echo Mode → 50% Echo Skill DMG Amp for 14s (lost on swap). Wishes of Quiet Snowfall + Glommoth gives incoming character 37% Glacio DMG Bonus from Outro. Letting It Go is a full-commitment finisher — cannot be cancelled, no swapping during cast. T0 tier in Tower of Adversity and Whimpering Wastes as of Patch 3.4. Sig weapon (Freeze Frame) gives 24% team-wide ATK buff — standard for modern Rectifier supports. Requires 3 different Echo sets for full flexibility — plan Echo farming accordingly.

### Hiyuki — Main DPS (Glacio)
**Source:** Prydwen.gg (Patch 3.3, profile update Jun 19, 2026)
- **Echo:** Reminiscence: Threnodian - Voidborne Construct | Wishes of Quiet Snowfall (5pc)
  - 4-cost main stat: CRIT Rate or CRIT DMG
  - 3-cost: Glacio DMG × 2 (or 1 Glacio DMG + 1 ATK% — close enough with sig)
  - 1-cost: ATK% × 2
- **Weapons:** Frostburn sig (best — massive CRIT Rate + Glacio Chafe + Liberation DMG; 100%) / Emerald of Genesis non-sig best (ATK 587, CRIT Rate 24.3%, 81.1%) / Blazing Brilliance alt (ATK 587, CRIT DMG 48.6%, 80.4%) / Feather Edge F2P BP (CRIT Rate secondary + ATK + Liberation DMG, 77.3%) / Fables of Wisdom F2P free (simple ATK, 72.4%). Sig gap is severe (~+19% over Emerald of Genesis) — high priority.
- **Stats:** ATK 1800–2200+, ER 120%, CR 65%+ before set (Wishes of Quiet Snowfall 5pc gives +25% CRIT Rate), CD 210–260%+, Glacio DMG 40–70%+
- **Forte priority:** Res. Liberation > Basic Attack > Forte Circuit > Res. Skill > Intro Skill (Intro skippable; Skill semi-skippable)
- **Substats:** ER (until 120%) > CD = CR (until 75% CR) > ATK% > Liberation DMG% > Flat ATK
- **Teams:**
  - Best Team: Hiyuki + Lucilla + Lynae + Chisa (Mornye only with Lynae; Shorekeeper/Verina as Chisa fallback)
  - Budget/Alt: Hiyuki + Lucilla/Zhezhi/Changli + Chisa + Shorekeeper/Verina
  - Last Resort: Hiyuki + Yinlin/Changli/Jianxin + Chisa + Support (significantly weaker without Lucilla/Lynae)
- **Rotation:**
  1. Summon Echo: Voidborne Construct (summon — use at any point)
  2. Intro: Frostedge (+1 Glacio Chafe, restores 200 Dedication)
  3. Basic Attack Present Self Stage 3 (restores 100–200 Dedication)
  4. Heavy Attack: Frost Splinter (3 arrows, cancel into Ult ASAP)
  5. Ultimate: Foreclaiming Inward Vision (enters Foreclaimed Self, grants 3 Frostharden Iai)
  6. Basic Foreclaimed 1/2/3 (cancel 3 via Skill)
  7. Skill: Jade Cleave → Skill: Petalfall
  8. Basic Foreclaimed 1/2/3 (cancel 3 via Dodge)
  9. Dodge → enter Iai Stance → Basic: Iai ×3 (each: 100 Frostheart + 1 Frostharden Iai consumed, 1 Whiteout Bitterfrost gained)
  10. Heavy Attack: Bitterfrost ×3 (each gains 1 Snowforged Blade)
  11. Hold Ultimate: Blade Liberation (consume all 3 Snowforged Blade for max DMG)
  12. Optional Skill before Outro (for ER)
  13. Outro
- **Sequences:** S1: Foreclaimed Self Basic multipliers +120%, Basic 3 pulls enemies, interrupt immunity on 4/5 — transformative damage and tankiness boost. S2: Iai DMG ×2.25, full Forte resource restore after leaving combat — massive Iai damage + rotation consistency. S3: Snow Rust auto-gains 1 extra stack every 2s (fixes solo play), Heavy ATK multipliers +160%, S2 Glacio Bite DMG at 2 stacks +488% multiplier. S4: Team +20% DMG for 30s on Skill cast + 18% HP restore. S6: Inward Vision + Blade Liberation CRIT DMG +500%; at 2 Snow Rust stacks, Glacio Bite triggers from ALL team Chafe applications — enormous. Priority: S1 first big breakpoint; S3 for solo content; S6 for whale territory.
- **Notes:** Fine Snow passive (Inherent Skill 1): Hiyuki gains Snow Rust stacks per Glacio Chafe/Havoc Bane applier on the team. 1 stack = Hiyuki's own Chafe; 2nd stack from Chisa (Havoc Bane) or Lucilla (Glacio Chafe) is the critical breakpoint — massively boosts Glacio Bite DMG. 3rd stack = +30% Glacio Bite Amp. Chisa is effectively mandatory for the 2nd Snow Rust stack. Glacio Chafe mechanic: all Chafe applied by any team member converts to Glacio Bite (Chafe type that deals significantly more DMG per stack + stuns 2s instead of freezing). Snowforged Blade management: can hold 3 stacks; save for strong enemy waves in multi-wave content by tapping instead of holding Blade Liberation. Very F2P unfriendly for optimal teambuilding — Lucilla, Lynae, and Chisa are all premium limited units. T0 DPS in Tower of Adversity as of Patch 3.3. Best Glacio DPS by a large margin in premium team. Outro: Glacio DMG dealt by nearby non-Hiyuki Resonators Amplified by 20% vs targets affected by Glacio Chafe for 20s.
