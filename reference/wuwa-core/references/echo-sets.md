## Common 3pc+2pc Combos

Many characters mix sets for optimal coverage. Common examples:
- `3pc [character set] + 2pc Moonlit Clouds` — for extra ATK buff from Outro
- `3pc [element set] + 2pc [element set]` — for pure element DMG
- `5pc [universal set]` — for specific mechanics (e.g. Brant Tidebreaking)

Always check the character's individual build entry for their recommended combo.
