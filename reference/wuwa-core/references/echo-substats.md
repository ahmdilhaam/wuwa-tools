# Echo Substat Ranges

**Source:** Chasey's WW Character Build Guide (~5300 sample dataset) | **Last Updated:** June 26, 2026

---

| Stat | Low | Mid-Low | Mid-High | High |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Crit Rate | 6.3–6.9% | 7.5–8.1% | 8.7–9.3% | 9.9–10.5% |

| Flat HP | 320–360 | 390–430 | 470–510 | 540–580 |
| Flat DEF | 40 | 50 | 60 | 70 |

---

## Roll Probability Notes

- **CR and CD** have different roll probability vs other substats — they are harder to hit
- The following substats share the same % roll chance: Basic%, Heavy%, Skill%, Liberation%, ATK%, HP%
- Flat ATK distribution: 30 (19% of rolls), 40 (42%), 50 (31%), 60 (8%) — 40 is most common

---

## Practical Use

When evaluating whether an echo is "good enough":
- CR roll of 9.9%+ = high tier
- CD roll of 19.8%+ = high tier
- Flat ATK 40 = average, 50+ = decent, 60 = excellent

These ranges are useful for the damage calculator and for telling viewers what to aim for when farming.
